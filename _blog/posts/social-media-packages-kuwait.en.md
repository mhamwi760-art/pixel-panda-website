## What a social media package actually is

A social media package is a fixed amount of content made for your brand every month, for a fixed price. Instead of paying per design, you agree on a monthly bundle: a set number of posts, stories, reels and photos, plus the planning that holds it all together.

A good package answers three questions before you pay:

1. **How much content do I get?** Numbers, not adjectives.
2. **What does it cost, and what is not included?** Ad budget is the big one.
3. **How does the month run?** Who plans, who approves, who publishes.

If a quote can't answer all three in writing, ask again before you sign anything.

## What should be included

Here is what we think every package in Kuwait should cover, whatever the price.

### Post designs
These are the main feed posts on Instagram, and often Facebook and LinkedIn too. They should be designed in your brand style, with your colours and fonts, not a generic template with your logo dropped on top.

### Story designs
Stories are where Gulf audiences spend a lot of their time. Offers, new arrivals, behind-the-scenes moments and polls all live here. A package should include a set number of designed story frames each month.

### Highlight covers
Highlights are the little circles under your bio. They turn your best stories into a permanent menu: Menu, Offers, Reviews, Delivery. Covers that match your brand make your profile look finished.

### Reels
Short video is where most new reach comes from right now. Even two or three reels a month make a difference. Check what "reel" means in the quote: filmed and edited, or edited from your own clips?

### Ad designs
If you plan to run paid ads, you need creative that is made for ads: a clear product, a short message and a strong first second. Note that designing an ad is not the same as running it. The ad budget is paid by you, directly to Meta, TikTok or Snapchat.

### Photography
Good photos are the base of everything else. A package that includes product or model photography saves you from reusing the same three pictures for months.

### A content calendar and captions
This is the part people forget to ask about. A content calendar shows what goes out, and when, before the month starts. And every design needs a caption. If captions cost extra, the "cheap" package is no longer cheap.

## What is usually not included

This is where hidden costs live, so read this part of any quote carefully.

- **Ad budget.** Always paid separately, straight to the platform.
- **Replying to comments and DMs.** Community management is a different job. Unless your package says so, you or your team still answer customers.
- **Raw and source files.** Most companies hand over finished designs, not the working files, unless agreed.
- **A new logo or brand identity.** That is a branding project, not a monthly package. If your brand doesn't have one yet, see our [branding packages](/branding.html).
- **Guaranteed results.** Nobody honest can promise a number of followers, sales or ad approvals. Be careful with anyone who does.

## Questions to ask before you sign

Whoever you work with in Kuwait or the GCC, ask these before you pay:

1. Can I see the full list of what is included, in numbers?
2. Are captions included for every design?
3. How many rounds of revisions do I get?
4. Who publishes the posts, you or me?
5. When does the month start, and what happens if I am late with approvals?
6. Can I see real work you've done for brands like mine?
7. Can I try before I commit?

That last question matters. It is the reason we offer **3 free sample designs** to every new brand, once, before you choose a package. You see our work on your brand first, and only then decide.
